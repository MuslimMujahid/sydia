# Subscription, trial, and storage

## Plan

- There is one plan, **Sydia Plus**, with every feature: chat on the web, WhatsApp, and Telegram; reminders; notes; files; calendar with Google Calendar; daily briefing. Chat is unlimited; there are no message quotas.
- Price: Rp59.000 per 30 days, currently shown with a promotional price on the subscription page. Quote the price the user sees on Pengaturan → Langganan when unsure; do not invent other prices.
- Prepaid for 30 days. Nothing renews or charges automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: access simply ends unless the user pays again.
- Storage: 10 GB in total, up to 100 MB per file. "Penyimpanan 10 GB penuh" means files must be deleted (Files page) before uploading more.

## Free trial

- Every new account gets a 7-day free trial once, starting automatically at sign-up. No card or payment is needed.
- The trial cannot be extended or restarted.

## When the trial or a paid period ends

- Nothing is deleted. Chats, notes, tasks, reminders, files, and contacts stay in the account.
- The account becomes read-only: the user can still open, read, download, and delete their data, but cannot add or change anything.
- Sydia stops replying in chat (web, WhatsApp, Telegram), and reminders and daily briefings stop being sent until the user subscribes again.
- After paying, everything continues where it left off.

## How to subscribe or renew

1. Open **Pengaturan → Langganan** (`/settings/billing`), or choose **Langganan** in the account menu.
2. Press **Berlangganan** ("Subscribe"; after expiry: **Berlangganan lagi**). This opens the order summary (`/checkout`).
3. Enter **Nomor telepon untuk tagihan** (billing phone number, with country code) and press **Berlangganan**.
4. Pay on the Mayar payment page: QRIS, e-wallets (GoPay, ShopeePay, DANA, LinkAja), bank transfer, card, or retail outlets, depending on what Mayar offers. The invoice is valid for 15 minutes; **Periksa pembayaran** rechecks a payment.
5. After payment, Sydia shows "Pembayaran berhasil" and the subscription is active. Receipts are under **Riwayat pembayaran** on the subscription page.

Renewal is possible only after the current period ends; periods do not stack. Before that, the page says the subscription is still active.

## Answering

- "Apakah Sydia berbayar?": yes after the 7-day free trial; give the plan, price, and what happens at expiry. Never say Sydia is free.
- The user's own trial or subscription end date is shown on Pengaturan → Langganan; Sydia cannot look it up in chat.
