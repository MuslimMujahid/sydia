# Web app pages

The web app's menus are in Indonesian. On a phone, the sidebar opens with the menu button (**Buka navigasi**).

Sidebar:

- **Chat baru** (`/`): chat with Sydia. Attach files with **Tambahkan file**, or pick notes and files as sources for a question. Earlier web chats are listed below the menu.
- **Kalender** (`/calendar`): calendar and tasks. The switch at the top toggles **Kalender** and **Tugas**. Views: Hari, Minggu, Bulan, Tahun, Jadwal. **Buat** → **Buat Acara** (event) or **Buat Tugas** (task). With Google Calendar connected, the event form chooses Google Calendar or Kalender Sydia.
- **Catatan** (`/notes`): notes, folders, tables, and **Catatan harian** (the daily note). **Buat baru** → **Buat folder baru**, **Buat catatan baru**, or **Buat tabel baru** (a table for spending, stock, readings). An item's menu: **Ubah nama**, **Pindahkan ke…**, **Salin tautan**, **Pindahkan ke Sampah**. **Sampah** (Trash) keeps items until **Hapus permanen**; **Pulihkan** restores them.
- **File** (`/files`): **Unggah file** uploads PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, RTF, text, Markdown, CSV, HTML, JSON, images, and audio, up to 100 MB each. Status: **Diproses** (processing), **Siap** (ready), **Gagal diproses** (failed; retry with **Coba ulang unggahan gagal**). A file's menu includes **Tanyakan di chat** to ask Sydia about it.
- **Lainnya** (More):
  - **Pengingat** (`/reminders`): all reminders. **Pengingat baru** creates one; filters Semua waktu, Hari ini, Mendatang, Sudah lewat. A reminder can repeat (**Pengulangan**: Harian, Mingguan, Bulanan, Tahunan, with an optional end) and can be **Penting** (important), which keeps reminding until confirmed.
  - **Kontak** (`/contacts`): contacts and contact groups (**Tambah kontak**, **Tambah grup**).
  - **Rahasia** (`/secrets`): encrypted secrets such as account numbers or Wi-Fi passwords. **Tambah rahasia** saves one; viewing needs the account password and hides it after 30 seconds. **Buat tautan ungkap** makes a one-time link to share a secret.

Account menu (the name/photo at the bottom of the sidebar): **Pengaturan** (Settings, opens Profil), **Langganan** (subscription and storage), **Keluar** (sign out).

Settings tabs: **Langganan** (`/settings/billing`), **Profil** (`/settings/profile`), **Asisten** (`/settings/assistant`), **Notifikasi** (`/settings/notifications`), **Integrasi** (`/settings/integrations`).

## Important reminders

A **Penting** reminder repeats every 5 minutes by default (5, 10, or 15 in the app; 5–60 when asked in chat), up to 10 messages, until the user confirms. Confirm by replying "oke", "sudah", or "done" to the reminder, or with **Konfirmasi** on the web. Snooze by replying "ingatkan 15 menit lagi", "nanti jam 3 sore", or "besok pagi".
